# Design brief: gotiadarian.com

Brief for a full design polish of the Goti Adarian website. Read the constraints first, then the focus areas.

## What this is

The website of Shri Pak Goti Adarian Saheb, a Parsi-Zoroastrian fire temple in Surat, Gujarat. The sacred fire is believed to be 600–700 years old; the first recorded reference is from 1796 CE. The site is maintained by volunteers for the community, for visiting families, and for people who want to learn about the faith.

**Tone:** reverent, calm, warm and dignified. Closer to a heritage institution than a charity campaign or a startup. Restraint reads as respect here. Gold is for emphasis, not decoration.

**Showing off the craft:** the aim is a polished, skilled UI/UX even though the site is static. Achieve it through typography, rhythm, spacing, motion and photography, not by adding features or content.

## Constraints (non-negotiable)

1. **Static export.** Next.js 15 with `output: "export"`, deployed to GitHub Pages (`web/`, built by `.github/workflows/nextjs.yml`). No server code, no API routes, no runtime data. Client-side JavaScript is fine, but keep it light.
2. **English only.** The Gujarati layer was removed on purpose. Do not reintroduce a language toggle.
3. **No invented content.** No placeholder text, stock photos, fake counts, fake testimonials, dummy links or `href="#"`. If a section needs content that does not exist, change the design so it does not need it, or leave it out. Do not fill it in.
4. **Real assets only** (listed below). If a design needs more photography, say so in the handoff notes rather than substituting stock imagery.
5. **Stack:** React 19, Tailwind CSS 3, framer-motion 11, Font Awesome 6.4 (CDN). Keep using Tailwind tokens from `web/tailwind.config.ts`.
6. **Accessibility:** keep the skip link, focus-visible outlines, `prefers-reduced-motion` handling, semantic landmarks, and AA contrast. Gold text on cream needs checking.
7. **Performance:** no remote images; optimise anything new (`node scripts/optimize-images.mjs` from `web/`). Keep First Load JS near the current ~147 kB.
8. **Mobile first.** Many visitors are on phones. Check 375 px width.

## Focus areas

### 1. Home page flow
Make the home page feel like a journey rather than a stack of cards.
- Hero: keep the single real photo. Refine the overlay, type scale and entrance motion; consider a scroll cue and a visible address or hours strip so a visitor sees "where and when" immediately.
- A more editorial intro (large serif pull-line, generous whitespace) instead of text next to three identical cards.
- Rework "Explore Our Heritage" so the three routes feel distinct, not three clones.
- A stronger, calmer donate band, plus the Yasna 28.4 quote as a quiet closing moment.
- Consider rhythm: alternate light, muted and navy sections deliberately and avoid every section having the same heading-plus-cards shape.

### 2. Trustees grid
- Six portraits in an editorial grid. Source photos vary in lighting and framing, so unify them with consistent crop, border and tone treatment (not heavy filters).
- Make the Panthaky block the standout: eight generations of the Turel family, with the lineage of priests presented elegantly, for example as a quiet list or timeline.
- Keep the names and roles exactly as written. See content notes below.

### 3. About timeline
- Turn the five-step history (Origins of Zoroastrianism → Migration to India → First Fire Temples → First Recorded Reference of Goti Adarian → Continuing Legacy) into a real vertical timeline with the Faravahar as a motif.
- Scroll-linked progress or staggered reveals are welcome; reduced-motion users must still get the full content.
- The three origin accounts (Khambhat Mobed, Fireball legend, Meteorite interpretation) deserve a clear "accounts, not facts" treatment, since the history says the true origin is uncertain.
- Vaspaan feature gets a considered layout of its own.

### 4. Global polish
- Type scale, line lengths, heading rhythm, and a consistent section-header pattern (optional small eyebrow label above titles).
- A motion system: one easing curve, one set of durations, subtle and purposeful. Currently `FadeIn` is 260 ms with an 8 px rise.
- Navbar: refine the scrolled state, active link indicator and mobile menu; the Donate item should be distinct without shouting.
- Footer: richer but calm; keep address, phone, hours and the map link.
- Buttons, cards, form fields and focus states as one coherent system. Check hover states on touch devices.
- Contact page: make the form and the "visit us" details feel like one welcoming moment; keep the Google Maps embed and map link.
- Page headers: give inner pages a more distinctive top than a plain muted band.
- Check 375 px, 768 px and 1280 px for every page.

## Content notes (confirm before treating as final)

These are things I noticed while auditing; they are not design problems but need an owner at the temple.
- **Generations of priests:** the history text says the Turel family served for **seven** generations, while the Trustees page says **eight**. The About page says seven. Decide which is correct and use it everywhere.
- **Spelling:** the history text writes "Adariyan" and "Dotivala"; the site writes "Adarian" and "Dotiwala". Pick one spelling and apply it everywhere.
- **Ryan / Rayan Modi:** the Trustees page uses "Rayan Modi" as the name and "Ryan Modi" in the alt text.
- **Rituals and zoroastrianism pages:** the prayer schedule, ceremony details and general faith content were carried over from the original site and have not been verified with the priests.
- **Dates on `/community`** are for 2026–27 and will need yearly updating.

## Out of scope

New pages, a blog or news section, online payments, a language toggle, a CMS, or anything that needs a backend.
